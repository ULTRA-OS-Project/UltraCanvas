- **The Language page previews numbers and dates.** Under the language and
  the translator, *Formats* shows what the language chosen writes - a
  number, a percentage, a file size, today's date in full and short, and
  the time - in its own words and the region's conventions, and whose
  conventions they are: this computer's regional settings when they are for
  that language or the choice is *System*, else the language's usual ones.
  It follows the dropdown as a language is picked.
