- **Edit a picture in UltraPaint, a drawing in ArtCreator, from UltraFiler.**
  Right-click a picture in UltraFiler on the TV and choose *Edit in
  UltraPaint*, or a drawing and choose *Edit in ArtCreator*: the editor opens
  it in a window beside it.
- **ArtCreator opens and saves drawings.** SVG, Illustrator, DXF and DWG,
  EMF and WMF, and OpenDocument drawings - before, ArtCreator in MobilePC
  could draw but said on Open and Save that it had no file formats.
