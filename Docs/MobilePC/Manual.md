## 1. What you need

- An Android phone or tablet (Android 9 or later).
- A TV or monitor, and either a USB-C to HDMI cable or dock - your phone must
  send video over USB-C (most Samsung Galaxy S and Tab models, recent Pixels
  and many others do) - or a wireless display your phone connects to as a
  second screen.
- Optional: a Bluetooth or USB mouse and keyboard.

## 2. Connect and start

1. Connect the phone to the TV or monitor.
2. Open **MobilePC**. The desktop appears on the TV; the phone shows the
   control pad and the line *The desktop is on the TV or monitor*.
3. Open **Apps** (on the pad, or the desktop's Apps button) and start an
   application: UltraFiler, UltraTexter, UltraViewer, UltraPaint or ArtCreator.
   UltraFiler also has its own button on the taskbar.

Disconnecting the TV brings the desktop to the phone's own screen; nothing is
closed. Connecting again moves it back.

### The phone's files

UltraFiler opens the phone's storage - the folders you see when the phone is
connected to a computer: Download, Pictures, DCIM, Documents. The first time
it starts it asks whether it may show all of the phone's files. Answer
**Yes**, and the phone's screen shows Android's switch for it (*Allow access
to manage all files*, or on Android 9 and 10 a permission question): switch
it on and come back to the control pad. Then open the folder again, or use
Refresh, and every file is there. Without it UltraFiler shows the folders but
few of the files in them. You can switch it on later in the phone's Settings,
under Apps > MobilePC.

## 3. The control pad

- **Touchpad:** move one finger to move the pointer on the TV. Tap to click.
  Tap with two fingers for the right button (the context menu). Move two
  fingers up or down to scroll. To drag something, tap, then press again and
  move.
- **Left click / Right click:** the buttons under the touchpad.
- **Keyboard:** brings up the phone's keyboard; it types into the window in
  front on the TV.
- **Apps:** opens the desktop's Apps window.

A mouse or a keyboard connected to the phone works directly; the pad is then
only needed for the phone's keyboard.

## 4. Working with windows

Every application opens in a window with a title bar:

- Drag the title bar to move the window; double-click it to maximise or
  restore it.
- The buttons at the right of the title bar minimise, maximise and close.
- Drag an edge or a corner to resize.
- The taskbar lists the open windows; clicking one brings it to the front.
- The phone's back button works as Escape: it closes a menu or a dialog.

## 5. When something goes wrong

| What happens | What to do |
|---|---|
| The TV stays black | Check that your phone sends video over USB-C, or that the wireless display connected as a second screen (not as mirroring). With mirroring, MobilePC shows the desktop on the phone and the TV shows the same. |
| The pointer does not move with the mouse | Tap MobilePC on the phone once: the mouse is taken over only while MobilePC is in front. |
| The phone's keyboard does not type into the window | Click the window on the TV first, so it is the one in front. |
| A file cannot be opened in ArtCreator | Opening and saving drawings is not available in MobilePC yet. |
