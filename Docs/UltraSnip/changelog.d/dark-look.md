- **UltraSnip follows the light or dark look.** The bar, the ground under the
  snip, the rules and the status line were fixed light colours; they are now
  the theme's, so a dark desktop gives a dark bar, and a change of look while
  UltraSnip runs is taken at once. An error in the status line uses the
  theme's warning red.
- **The settings window is dark too.** Its buttons were white with a dark
  icon in either look; they now use the theme's colours, and the window is
  built again in the new look, on the page it showed, when the look changes
  while it is open.
