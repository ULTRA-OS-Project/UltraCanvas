# Privacy declaration — www.ultraos.eu

**Version 0.1 (draft for legal review) · 21 October 2026**

This declaration explains what personal data is processed when you visit
**www.ultraos.eu**, the website of ULTRA OS Development GmbH, and what rights
you have under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It covers the
website only. The applications we publish each have a declaration of their
own, linked from section 8; using an application does not send us data
through this website.

> in, every block marked *Variant* must be kept or deleted according to what
> the site actually does, and the whole text must be reviewed by a lawyer

## 3. Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by [hosting provider, legal form, address, country],
which processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement
(Art. 28 GDPR). When you open a page, the web server records:

- the address of the page requested and the time of the request;
- the IP address of your device;
- the browser and operating system you use (the user-agent string);
- the page you came from, if your browser sends it (the referrer);
- the amount of data transferred and the HTTP status code.

**Purpose:** to deliver the pages you request, to keep the site secure (to
detect attacks and abuse) and to fix faults. **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f)
GDPR, our legitimate interest in a working and secure website. **Retention:**
log entries are deleted after [7 / 14 / 30] days unless an entry is needed as
evidence of an attack. **Transfer outside the EU:** [none — the servers are
in the EU / name the country and the safeguard, e.g. EU–US Data Privacy
Framework or standard contractual clauses].

## 5. Contact

**By e-mail.** When you write to us, we process your address, the content of
your message and whatever you attach, in order to answer you. **Legal basis:**
Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when your request concerns a contract or support for one of
our products, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our interest in answering
enquiries. **Retention:** we keep the correspondence as long as the matter is
open and afterwards as long as commercial and tax law require (§ 257 HGB,
§ 147 AO: six or ten years for business letters).

## 6. Downloads

*Variant A — served by our host.* Downloading an application from this site
is a page request like any other: the server log of section 3 records it.

*Variant B — served by GitHub.* Download links lead to our releases on GitHub
(GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA; in
the EU GitHub B.V., Netherlands). When you follow such a link, GitHub learns
your IP address and the file you requested, and may set its own cookies;
GitHub's privacy statement applies. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the
EU–US Data Privacy Framework. **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our
interest in distributing the software from the platform where it is built
and signed, and your interest in a verifiable download.

## 10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access (Art. 15), rectification
(Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data
portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21), in particular the right to
object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, and the right to withdraw a
consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). You also have the
right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (Art. 77); the
authority responsible for us is the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und
Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213
Düsseldorf, Germany.

To exercise a right, write to [privacy@ultraos.eu].
