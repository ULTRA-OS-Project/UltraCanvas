- **Copying: a steady time left, and the total number of items.** The time
  left is worked out from what is done and the time it took so far, so a copy
  of many small files no longer swings between seconds and hours. A copy of
  several files or folders shows Items and Size as "done / total".
- **Ctrl+C and Ctrl+X are confirmed on the status line:** "6 items copied to
  the clipboard", "\"Archive\" cut - paste it where it should go".
- **The right-click menu shows whole** when it fits the window, instead of a
  scrollbar - in search results it has one entry more.
- **Xara and CorelDRAW thumbnails show the file's own preview** - the picture
  Xara or CorelDRAW stored in it - instead of our own drawing of the file.
