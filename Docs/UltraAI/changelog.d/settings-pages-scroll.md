- **The settings window's pages scroll.** A page taller than the window was
  cut off at the bottom bar with no way to reach the rest: the Endpoints
  page lost its notes even at the window's full 800 px, and in a window
  made smaller every page lost its foot - at 480 px the Endpoints form
  stopped at "Default model", with the modes, *Save* and *Delete* out of
  reach. Each page now scrolls vertically when it needs to, as UOS-Settings'
  pages do, with the theme's scrollbar; a page that fits shows none and
  keeps its notes at the foot. Each page keeps its own place when another
  is shown.
- **The Endpoints form keeps to its width.** With a new endpoint open, the
  status beside *Save* ("Editing a new endpoint - fill in the form and
  Save.") took one line and widened the whole form past the page, so every
  field ran under the page's edge and *Delete* was cut off. The status
  wraps between the two buttons instead.
