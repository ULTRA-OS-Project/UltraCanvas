- **Your ChatCube ID can come back on a new computer.** In Edit profile, your
  relay confirms the e-mail address and the phone number with a code it
  sends to each; with both confirmed, *Turn recovery on* lets the relay keep
  a copy of your key. On another computer, *Recover my ChatCube ID* under the
  sign-in card asks for the relay and your e-mail or phone, then for the two
  codes, and signs in as you - same ID, same contacts - and you are told on
  both. Recovery is off until you turn it on, and the window says why: whoever
  runs the relay can read that copy. The relay needs e-mail and SMS set up
  for it (ChatCube relay 1.2.0); one that is not says so.
