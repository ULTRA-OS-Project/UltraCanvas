- **The look changes while ChatCube is open.** Choosing System, Light or
  Dark on the Settings window's Look page shows at once, in every ChatCube
  window - the main window, Settings and Edit profile - instead of at the
  next start; under System, ChatCube turns dark or light with the computer
  (its own mode, or the Look page of UOS-Settings) as it changes.
- **Dark look, everywhere**: the scroll bars of the conversation list, the
  messages, the Settings tree and the Edit profile page, the menus, the
  confirm and rename dialogs and the shared Proxy page take ChatCube's dark
  colours instead of light defaults.
- **Initials that read on every avatar**: the avatar colours of the light
  look are a shade deeper, so the white initials (and a sender's name in a
  group) stand out at 4.5:1; in the dark look the initials are dark on the
  lighter avatar colours. The Hang up button's words, too, read on its red in
  both looks.
