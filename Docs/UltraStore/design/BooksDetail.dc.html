When Hanne inherits her grandmother's garden on the dyke, the sea has already taken the first beds. Over three summers she learns what grows in salt, who stays in a village that is being moved inland, and which of her grandmother's stories were true. A quiet novel about keeping things, and letting them go.

Carla Brandt's reading won the audiobook prize of the Hamburg book fair; the e-book carries the author's six photographs of the real garden.
