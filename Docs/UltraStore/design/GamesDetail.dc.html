About this game

The sea took the streets, so the city moved up. Skyline Siege is turn-based tactics on rooftops, cranes and bridges: every unit has two actions a turn, height gives range, and cutting a district's power changes what the enemy can see. Thirty missions tell the story of one squad; the skirmish mode and the map editor keep going after that.

Two can play at one screen, each with a controller or one on the keyboard, in every mission and in skirmish. Saves are plain files in your home folder and can be moved between machines.
