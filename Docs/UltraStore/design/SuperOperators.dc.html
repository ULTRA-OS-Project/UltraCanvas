Operators

Who sits at this window, and what each may do. An admin makes the accounts and gives the roles; the services refuse what a role does not allow, and the audit log names the person, not the token. Built 2026-11-19: the marketplace service keeps the operator accounts (Server/UltraStoreMarket, POST /v1/operators/signin, /v1/operators); the session it signs reaches the account service with the same name and role. The window is the Operators page and the Settings page's sign-in (Apps/UltraStoreOperator/ui/OperatorOperatorsPage.cpp); the static operator token stays the bootstrap admin.

Sign in

An operator account at the marketplace service: the email and the password. The session it answers with is kept as the window's token, lasts the service's hours (12 by default) and is verified by the account service with the secret the two services share, so the Users page needs no token of its own. The static operator token the service was started with is the bootstrap admin: it makes the first admin account, then stays in a drawer.

Anyone changes their own name and password. A role gates a call at the service (403 forbidden, naming the role); the window hides the admin's controls from the others. A session ends by itself after the service's hours; a disabled account is refused from the next call on.
