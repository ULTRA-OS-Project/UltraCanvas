Why play from UltraStore

No launcher, no account

Install and press play. Games are plain ULTRA OS apps; there is nothing to sign into and nothing running in the background.

Signed and checked

Every game and every update is signed by the store and hashed; the age rating and the content descriptors are on the page before you install.

Controller, keyboard, touch

The page says which inputs a game supports. Gamepads work out of the box on every ULTRA OS machine, the Raspberry Pi included.

Small downloads, plays offline

Games share the UltraCanvas runtime, so a download is the game itself. Once installed, it plays without a connection.
