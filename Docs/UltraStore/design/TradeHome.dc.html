How trade assurance works

The order is a contract both sides sign in the store, and the money sits with the store’s payment service until the goods are in
1Agree the orderPrice, quantity, specification, lead time and the inspection terms are written into the order; both sides sign it in the store.
2Pay into escrowThe buyer pays the deposit, then the balance, to the store's payment service; the supplier sees it secured and starts production.
3Inspect and shipA pre-shipment inspection report and the shipping documents go into the order; the buyer releases the balance on receipt.
4DisputesLate, short or off-spec deliveries are claimed against the escrow within 30 days; the operator decides on the documents.
