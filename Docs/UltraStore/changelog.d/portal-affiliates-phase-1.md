- **UltraAffiliates, phase 1: product links** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.10.0, its README's *Affiliates*; `Docs/Research/UltraAffiliatesDesignProposal.md`). Off unless `ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_AFFILIATES=1`.
  - **Free for the publisher, and every paid app is in it.** An affiliate's link to an app - `/r/<handle>/<app>/` - earns 5 % of the net price of the sale it brings, paid out of the store's fee; the publisher's share is untouched. A publisher's owner may add a boost of 1 to 45 % on an app, a category or everything, which comes out of the publisher's proceeds of that sale and of no other.
  - **The last link to the product wins it, for 90 days**, when it was followed by a person - a page fetched as a picture or in a frame counts nothing. The buyer themselves, the seller's own people and an affiliate who is not active earn nothing.
  - **A superaffiliate** - whoever's recruiting link brought an affiliate in - earns 10 % of that affiliate's commissions on top, at the store's cost, one level up and no further.
  - **Pending for 30 days, then approved**; a refund or a chargeback takes a commission, the superaffiliate's share and the publisher's boost back in the proportion of the money.
  - **An affiliate is a person the store knows**: a phone number proven by a texted code (or confirmed by the operator where the portal has no text gateway), and an identity document the operator has seen and written down - no picture is kept, and one person has one profile.
  - **Blocked or banned** affiliates earn nothing; each case has a record that is only ever added to, which the affiliate sees the reasons of at once and can answer.
  - The affiliate's pages, the publisher's *Affiliates* page and the operator's; `ultrastore-portal affiliates maintain` for the daily work. The portal moves no money: the operator pays an affiliate and writes the reference down.
  - Not in this phase: the binding of a customer to an affiliate, the holdback, statements, codes, fixed boosts for hardware, the native store's links.
