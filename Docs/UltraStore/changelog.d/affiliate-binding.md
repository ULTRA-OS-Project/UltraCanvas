- **UltraAffiliates, phase 2: a customer an affiliate brought stays theirs** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.11.0, its README's *Affiliates*; `Docs/Research/UltraAffiliatesDesignProposal.md` §8). Until now a link earned the one sale it led to, and a link to the shop earned nothing.
  - **A new customer is bound to the affiliate whose link they followed** - at sign-up, or at their first paid order - when the link is no older than 90 days and they have never paid for anything. Every later purchase and every renewal of theirs earns that affiliate the base, 5 % of the net price out of the store's fee, **and no boost**: a publisher's boost is for the link that sold the product.
  - **Another affiliate's link to a product wins that product and moves nothing**: that affiliate earns base and boost on the sale, the customer stays bound where they were. One sale, one commission.
  - **The win-back**: a customer who paid for nothing in six months and buys within 14 days of following an affiliate's link becomes that affiliate's, whether they were another's or nobody's (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_AFFILIATE_WINBACK_MONTHS`, `_WINBACK_DAYS`). Nothing else moves a binding.
  - **The binding follows the customer's account**: suspended while it is closed or its deletion is asked for, back with it, removed when it is erased - the commissions it earned stay. A ban or the erasure of the affiliate ends it; nobody is their own customer.
  - **A referred customer who becomes an affiliate has their affiliate as superaffiliate**, whatever recruiting link they applied through.
  - **An order knows the account that bought** (`Order.buyer`): whoever is signed in on the buy page, the person of the access token sent as `Authorization: Bearer` with `POST /v1/checkout`, and a guest's order once its owner opens it signed in under the same address. `POST /v1/checkout` takes `via`, the last link followed, beside `ref`.
  - The account page says *Who referred you*; an affiliate sees how many customers they have and each sale as "your customer no. N", never who.
  - Not reached yet: purchases made in the native store, which neither signs in at the portal nor sends a link, and guests' purchases - they bind nobody.
