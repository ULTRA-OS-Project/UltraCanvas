- **UltraAffiliates, phase 3, the money: 5 % held back for six months, and a payout a month from 25.00** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.12.0, its README's *Affiliates*; `Docs/Research/UltraAffiliatesDesignProposal.md` §8). Until now an approved commission was owed whole at once, and the operator wrote a payout down when it was paid.
  - **The holdback.** When a commission is approved, 5 % of what is left of it goes into the holdback until six months after the sale was paid (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_AFFILIATE_HOLDBACK_PERCENT`, `_HOLDBACK_MONTHS`): of 10.00, 9.50 are in the balance after 30 days and 0.50 follow. A refund or a chargeback in that time comes out of the holdback first, then off the balance; on its day what is left is released. The superaffiliate's share is held like its commission.
  - **The statistics show it**: per currency the amount held and the next release with its day, beside pending, balance, what is on its way and what was paid; each line says what is held of it and until when.
  - **A payout a month, per currency, from 25.00** (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_AFFILIATE_PAYOUT_MINIMUM`): the approved commissions less their holdback, what was given back, and the holdbacks released, due on a day. A smaller balance is carried to the next month. An affiliate whose account is closed or being deleted is paid whatever is owed.
  - **Made, then paid.** *Make the payouts* on the new `/operator/affiliates/payouts/`, or `ultrastore-portal affiliates payouts` from a monthly timer, writes what to pay and where; the operator pays and marks each paid with the transfer's reference. The portal moves no money.
  - **A payout is the affiliate's statement** (`/affiliate/payouts/<no>/`): each commission with what was held back of it, what was given back, the holdbacks released, and the sum. A statement, not a tax document.
  - A blocked or banned affiliate's money waits: nothing is approved, released or paid, a payout made before the block included.
  - `affiliates maintain` now also releases holdbacks; a payout written down before this version is marked paid, and commissions approved before it have no holdback.
  - Not in it: the self-billed credit note, paying through the provider's interface, a ban's settlement in the portal, fixed boosts for hardware, the native store's links.
