- **VAT, tax invoices and payouts** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.9.0, its README's *Buying*). A receipt said of itself that it was "not a tax invoice", the store's fee was reckoned on a price with the tax still in it, and nothing said what a publisher had been paid. All three are off until the store is configured as the seller (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_SELLER_NAME`, `SELLER_ADDRESS`, `SELLER_VAT_ID`, `SELLER_COUNTRY`); without them the portal behaves as 0.8.0.
  - **The tax comes out of the price**, at the rate of the buyer's country on the day the money moves; the fee is 15 % of the net and the publisher's share the rest (19.90 at 19 %: 3.18 tax, 2.51 fee, 14.21 share). The buy page asks for the country, a client sends `country` with the checkout, and with none it is the seller's. Outside the EU no EU tax is charged, and the payment says so.
  - **The rates are the operator's**, on the new `/operator/tax/`: a rate for a country and a day, replaced when entered again, a later day being a change to come. `ultrastore-portal money seed-rates` loads a starting table marked *to be checked* - the sources consulted disagreed on two countries, so it is loaded by hand and not by a migration. A member state with no rate is charged the seller's, and the tax page counts those payments.
  - **An invoice for every payment and a credit note for every refund and chargeback**, in one series without gaps, each a copy of what it says as it stood. The buyer's *Receipt* link shows it; a credit note names the invoice it takes back and returns the tax in the proportion of the money.
  - **A quarter's net, tax and total by country and rate**, on the tax page and from `money quarter`.
  - **Payouts.** A sale's share is due 30 days after it was paid, what is given back comes off at once, and a sum under the minimum is carried - so a refund after a payout comes off the next one. *Make the payouts* on `/operator/payouts/` (or `money payouts`) writes them; **the portal moves no money**: the operator pays and marks a payout paid with the transfer's reference. The publisher's owner - and nobody else in the publisher - says where the share goes; `/console/sales/` shows what is not paid out yet and every payout with its statement.
  - Correcting a rate for the same country and day was refused as "already exists" by the first form; the test found it, and the form no longer checks what the page is there to replace.
  - Left for a tax adviser and a later version: the reverse charge for business buyers, the evidence of a buyer's country beyond what they said, taxes outside the EU, paying through the provider's interface, and the self-billed document a payout is to a publisher.
