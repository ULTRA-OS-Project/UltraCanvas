- **The store's fee and the publisher's share of a sale** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.7.0, its README's *Buying*). Until now the portal recorded what a buyer paid and nothing about who it belonged to: `/console/sales/` said so itself ("before the payment provider's fees and the store's share"), and its README listed the split as not built.
  - The store keeps **15 %** of every payment (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_STORE_FEE_PERCENT`, from 0 to under 100; a value outside that, or not a number, stops the portal at start) and the rest is the publisher's. The split is written on the payment when the money moves (`Payment.fee_percent`, `store_fee`; `billing/split.py`), to the cent, half up, and never reckoned again: a fee changed later leaves earlier sales as they were.
  - A refund and a chargeback give the fee back in the proportion of the money given back, and the last cent of a payment returns whatever of its fee is left, so a payment refunded in parts nets to nothing on both sides (19.90 at 15 %: fee 2.99; 5.00 back returns 0.75, the other 14.90 the remaining 2.24). A renewal is split like a sale.
  - `/console/sales/` shows each payment with the store's fee and the publisher's share, and *Your share* per currency in place of the net alone; the operator's order page shows the same with the percentage each payment was sold under.
  - Payments recorded before get the split when the database is migrated (`billing/migrations/0002_payment_split.py`), at the fee configured then.
  - Not in it: VAT (the fee is reckoned on the amount paid until the portal knows the tax in it), the payment provider's fee, invoices and payouts.
