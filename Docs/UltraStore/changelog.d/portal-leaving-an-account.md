- **An account can be closed, deleted and brought back** (`Server/UltraStorePortal` 0.8.0, its README's *Leaving an account*). The portal had no way to leave at all: the account service it replaces deleted an account after a grace, and the portal, which is every service's sign-in, had neither a page nor a line of code for it.
  - **Closed**, by the person with the password: signed out everywhere, every service's access ended, nothing signs in. The account is kept whole for a year (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_ACCOUNT_CLOSED_DAYS`, 365). Signing in with the right password in that time offers *Reactivate my account*, and it is as it was left; a forgotten password can still be reset. A wrong password says what it always says, so only the owner learns the account is closed.
  - **Deleting**, asked for the same way: the account stays usable for 90 days (`ULTRASTORE_PORTAL_ACCOUNT_DELETION_GRACE_DAYS`), its page says the day it goes, and *Keep my account* takes the request back.
  - **Erased**, when the days are over: the row stays under its id with the address replaced and free again, the name and the password gone, every grant, token, sign-in, consent and membership removed, and the audit rows it acted in naming `account <id>`. `ultrastore-portal accounts maintain` does it, from a daily timer, and is safe to run again; `accounts leaving` lists who is on the way out, by id.
  - A mail goes out at a closing and at a deletion request, with the day and the way back. An operator and the owner of a publisher cannot leave this way, and the page says why. Orders and receipts are not the account's and stay.
