- **The store tells the payment service where the buyer is.** The price includes the VAT of the buyer's country, and the checkout sent none: every purchase made in the store was taxed at the seller's rate, whoever bought. `POST /v1/checkout` now carries `country` - the first of these that is a country (`BuyerCountry`, `Purchase.h`): a new **Country** field on the Settings page (`purchase.country`), the country of the account the store is signed in to, the region of the system's language (`de-AT` is Austria; `zh-Hant-TW` is Taiwan, the script is not the country; `es-419` names none). The last is a guess, so the field shows what an empty one gives and anyone can set it right. With none of the three nothing is sent and the service reckons with the seller's, as before. `ultrastore buy ... --country <CC>` on the command line. The two new texts are in all 29 languages.
