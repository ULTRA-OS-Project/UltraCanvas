- **The text bar's font list is the framework's font picker.** It lists the
  fonts the image's text layers use under *In this document*, the fonts chosen
  last under *Recent* (kept in UltraPaint's settings, `text.recentfonts`), then
  every installed font, each drawn in itself with its format's icon; a family
  with Light, Condensed or Black styles opens them beside it, and the one
  chosen sets the font with Bold and Italic. One Escape closes one list
  (framework, *One Escape closed two menus*).
