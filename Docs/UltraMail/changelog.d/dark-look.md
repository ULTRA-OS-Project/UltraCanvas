- **UltraMail follows a dark desktop everywhere, and a change of the look
  while it is open.** The folder, message-list and reading-pane cards, the
  text fields, the account settings, the composer's attachment chips, the
  contact manager's list, the outbox and the Settings window (which was a
  light window on any desktop) take the desktop's colours; buttons put the
  look's text on the accent and on the red of *Delete account*, so their
  labels read in both looks; dialog icons and the paperclip are drawn in the
  look's ink instead of black. Switching the desktop (or `Appearance.conf`)
  between light and dark restyles every open UltraMail window at once - the
  main window, the composer, the contact manager, Settings and the dialogs -
  where before the hand-set colours kept the look UltraMail was started in,
  and labels lost their sizes.
- **A message's HTML stays on its own white page.** A formatted message keeps
  the sender's colours on a white page - on a dark desktop a framed sheet
  inside the dark reading pane - instead of the sender's dark text on the
  dark pane. The composer's and the signature editor's formatted body is the
  same white page, so the colours picked in the formatting bar (made for a
  white page) stay readable while writing; the plain-text body follows the
  look.
