- **New design proposal: UltraAffiliates, one affiliate programme for every
  ULTRA OS shop and marketplace** (`Docs/Research/UltraAffiliatesDesignProposal.md`).
  Until now there was a two-tier, code-based scheme for the app store only,
  run from the merchant's desktop (`UltraStoreAdmin`), and the portal that
  replaces it keeps an order's code without crediting anyone. The proposal
  makes the operator the network between merchants and affiliates. Merchants
  publish programmes with a percentage or a fixed commission per product, and
  affiliates browse them and take links. A new customer is bound for good to
  the affiliate whose link brought them and earns that affiliate a commission
  on every later purchase in every shop, from the merchant's rule or, where
  there is none, from a base rate the operator pays. A link to one product
  wins that product's commission for 30 days without moving the binding. The
  binding is suspended while the customer's account is closed, restored on
  reactivation and ends with the account's erasure. The proposal also covers
  commissions from pending to paid, self-billed credit notes, fraud
  defences, the questions for a lawyer, four phases and ten decisions for
  the owner.
