- **New design proposal: UltraAds, the advertising service of the ULTRA OS
  shops and services** (`Docs/Research/UltraAdsDesignProposal.md`). Nothing
  for advertising existed beyond one *Sponsored* mark drawn in the
  marketplace's search page. The proposal answers whether a shop sells
  advertising for itself or for every area - both, from one form: a *Promote*
  button on each listing that starts in that shop and can be widened, and one
  console, one balance and one review behind every shop - and what one
  registration for all shops and services takes: the ULTRA account as an
  OpenID Connect provider, with advertising a capability of the publisher a
  merchant already is. An advertisement promotes a listing, is chosen from
  what is on the page and never from who is looking, and is always marked;
  the editor's picks are not for sale and nothing is shown in the system or
  its applications. With the placements per area, the functions in three
  priorities, the data model as an app of the store portal, the roadmap, and
  twelve questions for the owner. No code.
