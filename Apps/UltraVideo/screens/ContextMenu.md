Right-click menus for clips, transitions, the music bar and empty lane space, built on the framework menu element.

**Consumer provides:** the items for the target under the pointer, with icons, shortcuts and enabled states.

- A muted header names the target. Group items with separators, and put destructive items last in `danger`.
- The hover row uses `accent-soft`. Submenus open on the right with a `›` hint.
- Every item has a keyboard shortcut or equivalent elsewhere.
