A badge over the cut between two clips. Its width equals the transition length, and it shows the type icon and the length.

**Consumer provides:** the cut time, the length, the type and the engine's maximum length.

- `transition-fill` with `on-transition` is inverted against the theme, so it reads over any clip. The minimum width is 22 px.
- Selected: a 2 px `accent` ring and 4 px `accent` edges. Dragging an edge sets the length and shows the readout "2.0 s · max 2.0 s".
- Drop a transition from the bin onto a cut: a `focus` insertion line and a translucent badge preview appear. Double-click to change the type; `Del` reverts to a cut.
