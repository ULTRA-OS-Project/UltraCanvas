Affiliates

Affiliates advertise what the store sells and are paid when their link brings a sale. It costs you nothing, and there is nothing to switch on: every paid app is in it, and the store pays an affiliate {{ base_percent }} % of the net price out of its own fee.

To have something sold, add a boost: more commission on your apps, from 1 to 45 % of the net price. A boost comes out of your proceeds, and only of a sale an affiliate's link to that app brought.
